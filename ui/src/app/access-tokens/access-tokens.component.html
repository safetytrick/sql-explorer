How a token's access is decided

A token may do what both its ceiling and you may do, checked on every request. Your access is taken from the groups you had at your latest sign-in: if a group is taken away from you, your tokens lose it when you next sign in, not before. That is why every token expires, at most {{list.maxLifetimeDays}} days after it is made. Revoke a token you no longer need.

Anything a token writes, a tag say, is recorded as yours. A token cannot make or revoke tokens.
